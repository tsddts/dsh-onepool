fix: rewrite the git-context fingerprint in all three lanes

A conversation whose assistant history quotes
`Main branch (you will usually use this for PRs)` fails every later request
with 400 / 11128, permanently: the gateway scans message CONTENT, the quote is
now part of the history, and resending, switching accounts or restarting all
fail because the request body itself is what is rejected.

Upstream rewrote that sentence in one of the three paths that serialise a
message — the channel lane's CONTENT_FINGERPRINTS. The pool lane
(BANNED_SYSTEM_PHRASES) and BuddyAdapter's inline chain did not, so on
2026-11-18 an explanation of the filter quoted the filter and killed its own
session: turns 5 and 6 both INVALID_REQUEST.

Both missing sites now carry the rule. Measured against copilot.tencent.com
with a pool credential: the failing session's assistant text answers
400 / 11128 as-is and clears channel verification after the rewrite.
